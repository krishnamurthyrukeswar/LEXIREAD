Privacy Policy for LexiRead

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

LexiRead processes text-based material on-device. Imported books, reading progress, bookmarks, notes, highlights, and dictionary cache data are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

This local build does not enable Firebase, analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, Google Drive import, or AI assistant services. If cloud features are added later, this policy should be updated before release.

3. Local Storage

Unsynchronized books, metrics, and offline DBs (like the Offline Dictionary) remain exclusively on your device within secured app sandboxes.

4. Contact Us

For any queries about data tracking, please email contact@lexiread.app.
